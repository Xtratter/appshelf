AppShelf keeps a list of the apps installed on your phone — name, package, where each one came from and when it was installed — so you can reinstall them after a factory reset or on a new phone.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>All installed apps sorted by name, with letter headers</li>
<li>Source of every app: Google Play, F-Droid, Aurora Store, Obtainium, RuStore, GetApps, Galaxy Store, AppGallery, Amazon — or an APK file, including the app it was opened from</li>
<li>Install date, version and last update</li>
<li>Search, filter by source, optional system apps</li>
<li>Save the list as JSON, Markdown or CSV, or share it as text</li>
<li>Autosave to a file you choose every time you open the app</li>
<li>Restore mode: open a saved list, see which apps are missing and install each from its store</li>
<li>Material 3 "liquid glass" design, five themes including AMOLED black and light</li>
</ul>

No internet permission: the list never leaves the phone unless you save or share it yourself.
